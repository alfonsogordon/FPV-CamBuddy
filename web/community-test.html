Who should test?

GoPro testers are absolutely wanted, along with DJI Action / Osmo, Insta360, Sony, Blackmagic and Caddx users. Results from cameras that fail are useful too.

For GoPro, please test native saved-preset discovery plus LOW / MIDDLE / HIGH switching. DJI Action 4 / 5 Pro / 6 can test the currently exposed Video, Slow Motion, Timelapse, Hyperlapse and Photo modes. DJI native Custom Modes 1–5 are not currently claimed as supported.

Quick test

  • Camera discovers and connects.
  • Settings read, save and survive a reboot.
  • ARM starts recording; DISARM stops it. Repeat 5 times.
  • Camera reconnects after camera and CamBuddy power cycles.
  • Check available telemetry: recording state, battery, mode, recording time and storage.

Profile / Mode Switch + OSD

The LOW / MIDDLE / HIGH Profile Switch isn't GoPro-only; CamBuddy uses the supported mechanism available for each camera.

  • GoPro: discover native saved presets, assign LOW / MIDDLE / HIGH and test all three.
  • Other supported cameras: test any profiles/modes CamBuddy exposes.
  • Confirm the camera itself changes to the expected profile/mode.
  • Enable Show profile name in OSD; check the correct name appears for about 2.5 seconds and normal OSD returns.
  • Test the Display in destination.
  • Turn Profile OSD OFF; switching should still work without the message.
  • Reboot and confirm assignments are retained.

AP / Field Configurator

  • Enable/open the CamBuddy AP and connect your phone to FPVCamBuddy.
  • Open http://cambuddy.local and confirm the configurator loads.
  • Also test http://192.168.4.1 as the fixed fallback.
  • Check saved settings, change one setting and save it.
  • Reboot CamBuddy and confirm the change is retained.

cambuddy.local is new in this candidate and needs real-device testing; 192.168.4.1 remains the fallback.
